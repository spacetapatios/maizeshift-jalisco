Method & data

NASA POWER Monthly 2 m air temperature (T2M) and bias-corrected precipitation (PRECTOTCORR), community “AG”, fetched live from the NASA POWER API for the selected point.

Growing season = June–October (typical rainfed maize cycle in Jalisco). Earlier period = 1995–2009, recent period = 2010–2025. Trend = least-squares line. Rainfall = daily rate × days in month, summed over the season.

Limitations: one grid point per region (about 0.5° × 0.625° cells), no crop-yield data, no irrigation or soil data. Next step: add SMAP soil moisture and MODIS/Landsat vegetation (NDVI).
